
# Family Hand & Foot – Complete Rules

## 1. Setup
- **Deck Count**: Use `(number_of_players + 1)` standard 52-card decks (including Jokers).  
  - Example: 2 players → 3 decks; 4 players → 5 decks.
- **Dealing**:
  - Each player starts with two piles:
    - **Hand**: 11 cards (held in hand).
    - **Foot**: 11 cards (face-down, revealed when hand is empty).
- **Wild Cards**:
  - Jokers (and any extra printed/advertisement cards) are wild.
  - 2s are also wild.
- **Wild Card Values**:
  - Joker or extra wild = **50 points**.
  - 2 = **20 points**.

---

## 2. Card Point Values

| Card Rank         | Points |
|-------------------|--------|
| 4–8               | 5      |
| 9–K               | 10     |
| Ace               | 20     |
| 2 (wild)          | 20     |
| Joker/extra wild  | 50     |
| Black 3           | -5     |
| Red 3             | -300   |

**Notes:**
- Negative points apply if cards remain in hand/foot at end of round.
- Red 3 penalty applies only if still held when round ends.

---

## 3. Play Down Rules
- **Initial Requirement**:
  - Round 1: Must lay down cards totaling at least **60 points** from your hand in a single turn.
  - Round 2: Requirement = **90 points**.
  - Each subsequent round: +30 points.
- **Wild Card Restriction**:
  - At all times, no meld (book) may contain more wilds than natural cards.
- **Important**: Points for playing down come only from the cards you lay down in that turn, not from your total hand.

---

## 4. Turn Sequence
1. **Draw Phase**  
   - **Default:** Draw **two cards** from the draw pile.  
   - **Special Unlock:** If you’ve already met the play-down requirement for the round (60, 90, 120, etc.) **and** you have **two natural cards** in hand that match the **top card of the discard pile**:  
     1. Lay down the two matching cards **plus** the top discard as a meld (no wilds allowed for this unlock).  
     2. Take the top discard **and the next 5 cards** from the discard pile into your hand.  

2. **Meld/Book Phase**  
   - If you haven’t played down yet, you must lay down cards from your hand worth at least the **current round’s point requirement** in one turn to start your books.  
   - Once you’ve played down:  
     - You may add cards to **only your own** existing melds/books.  
     - You may start new melds if you have 3+ of the same card.  
     - You cannot play on another player’s books.  

3. **Discard Phase**  
   - Discard **exactly one** card to end your turn.  
   - Discards are placed face-up in a pile; only the top card is visible.  

---

## 5. Discard Rules
- Discards are stacked face-up; only the top card is visible.
- **Unlock Rule**:
  1. If the previous player discards a card you have at least **two matching natural cards** of in your hand:
     - Immediately play your matching natural cards down (no wilds allowed for this unlock).
     - Take the top discarded card.
     - Draw 5 additional cards from the **draw pile**.
  2. If fewer than 5 cards remain in draw pile, take what’s left.

---

## 6. Books
- **Clean Book**: All natural cards, no wilds.
- **Dirty Book**: Contains wilds, but never more wilds than natural cards.
- **Book Size**:
  - Complete at 7 cards but may contain more.
- **End-Game Requirement**:
  - Must have at least 1 clean book and 1 dirty book to go out.

---

## 7. Moving to the Foot
- When your hand is empty, turn over your foot pile and continue playing.
- Must finish playing your foot before going out.

---

## 8. Ending the Round
- **Conditions to Go Out**:
  - At least 1 clean book.
  - At least 1 dirty book.
  - No cards left in hand or foot (discard optional — you may play all remaining cards).
- **Bonus**:
  - Player who goes out first gets +100 points.
  - All others get one more turn.

---

## 9. Scoring
- **Positive Points**:
  - Cards in completed books and melds on table score their value.
- **Negative Points**:
  - Cards left in hand or foot count as negative points.
  - Black 3 = -5 points each.
  - Red 3 = -300 points each (only if still held at round end).

---

## 10. Example Turn
1. You start your turn with:
   - 3 Kings = 3 × 10 = **30 points**
   - 2 Eights = 2 × 5 = **10 points**
   - 1 Wild 2 = **20 points**
2. You lay them down in one turn: **Total = 60 points** → enough to start books in Round 1.
3. Once down, you can add more cards to your own books in future turns to score more points.

---

## 11. Strategy Tips
- Discard red 3s as soon as possible to avoid -300 point penalty.
- Track cards in discard pile to use the unlock rule effectively.
- Save wilds for high-value plays but never exceed the natural card count in a meld.
